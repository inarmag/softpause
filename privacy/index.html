Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 (softPause 1.1)

In short

softPause has no account and no server of its own. Nothing you record leaves your devices: your levels, your pauses and your reminders stay on your phone, and on your Apple Watch if you use softPause there. Two things can leave them: a purchase of softPause+, and anonymous usage events, only if you say “Share”.

On your devices only

softPause keeps these on your phone, and never sends them anywhere:

  • How you felt before and after each pause, and the pauses themselves (when, which breath, how long)
  • What softPause has noticed in them, and the one word you may add after a pause
  • Your reminders and their times
  • Your settings: breath holds, haptics, the companion you breathe with and any name you give it
  • The story pictures you make, until you share them yourself

Apple Watch. If you use softPause on an Apple Watch, the breaths and your pauses pass directly between your iPhone and your watch, over Apple's own connection between the two, and are kept on those two devices only. Nothing goes through a server of ours.

Someone you trust. “Not okay?” can open your phone's contact picker so you can call someone. softPause reads only the one contact you pick, on the phone, to call them; it keeps nothing and sends nothing.

1. Purchases

If you buy softPause+, Apple (or Google) handles the payment. We use Adapty to know that you bought it, under a random ID that isn't your name or email. The app tells Adapty not to collect your IP address or your advertising ID; Adapty's software does receive the ID your phone gives this app (not the advertising ID) when it first starts. Adapty stores this record in the United States, under its data processing agreement with us.

2. Anonymous usage events, only if you say “Share”

At the end of the first run, and in Your data, softPause asks whether to share anonymous usage events. They are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off anytime in Your data. If you share them, PostHog (in the United States) receives which screens are used, whether the paywall was seen or a purchase made, when SOS or reminders are used, and when the app crashes, under a random ID made on your phone. Never your levels, your answers, your pauses or anything from Your pauses. The same random ID lets us see whether a purchase followed, so we learn what helps people decide.

Different versions

softPause may show different versions of some screens, such as the first run or the paywall, to learn what works better. The version is chosen on your phone, or by Adapty under your purchase ID; nothing about how you feel is used to choose it.

Sharing a pause

When you share a pause, from the Close or from Your pauses, the picture is drawn on your phone, and the line you may write on it is never kept. It goes only where you send it: through your phone's share sheet, or straight into Instagram Stories, where Meta's own policy applies. For a story, the App Store or Google Play link (or your gift's) is put on your phone's clipboard for you to paste, nowhere else. Save adds the picture to your Photos: softPause asks only to add photos, and never reads them. softPause uploads nothing and never learns whether you posted it.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS or Android. softPause sends no notification from a server and does not see when a reminder arrives.

Deleting everything

Your data → Delete all data erases everything softPause keeps on this phone, and uninstalling the app does the same. Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account and can be restored. To ask Adapty or PostHog to remove what they hold under your random ID, write to us and we will do it.

Children

softPause is meant for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect anything from children under 13; if you think a child has used softPause+ or shared usage events, contact us and we will help remove it.
