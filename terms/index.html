What softPause is

softPause is a breathing practice for moments of stress. It suggests a breath and paces you through it. It is a breathing practice, not medical care, and it does not replace a doctor or emergency services.

If you have trouble breathing, chest pain or feel faint, or you are thinking about harming yourself, stop and get urgent help: use Not okay? in the app or call your local emergency number. If a breath feels uncomfortable, stop and breathe normally.
