Nothing to keep up

A pause is for the moment you’re not okay. That is not the moment to be shown a number, and it shouldn’t be one more thing to keep up with.

So softPause keeps nothing to keep up

  • No streaks, no daily goals, no count of days.
  • No badges that can be lost, and nothing that can go down.
  • No account, and no server of ours holding your pauses.
  • A record that only ever grows: pauses so far, and how many ended lower than they began.
  • A companion that never gets sad when you’re away.

Come back after a week, or after a month: nothing is lost, and nothing is late. softPause asks what it always asks, how strong is it right now, and picks a breath.

Why it matters to us

Many wellness apps turn care into another thing to keep up with: a flame to protect, a chain of days not to break, a pet that looks sad when you miss one. For some people that helps. Others end up tired of it, and it even has a name, streak fatigue; in September 2026 it was written about in the press.

We would rather you open softPause because a breath helped last time, not because you are afraid of losing something. If you need it twice today and then not again until spring, that is exactly how it’s meant to be used.

Your record stays yours

Your pauses stay on your phone. We have no copy of them, and no count of your days to show anyone. The privacy policy says exactly what can leave the phone.

Come back whenever you need it.
