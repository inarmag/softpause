For clinicians

For therapists, psychologists, coaches and GPs whose clients and patients get anxious, most often between two appointments.

What softPause is

A breathing app for the moment it gets hard. The person says how they feel right now, on five levels from Overwhelmed to Okay. softPause picks one matched breath (a few sighs, cyclic sighing or slow breathing) and paces it with a glowing orb and soft haptics. Then the same question again: how do they feel now?

No account, no email, no sign-up. Their pauses stay on their phone, and it works offline.

What it is not

softPause is a breathing practice, not medical care. It is not a medical device, it makes no diagnosis, and it does not stand in for you. Slow breathing has been studied, and many people find a few slow breaths make a hard moment easier to get through. We claim nothing more than that.

Why it fits your practice

  • Between sessions. A pause takes from under a minute to a few, at the moment it is needed, wherever the person is.
  • Their own record. Every pause keeps a before → after pair, on the same five-step scale. Nothing on it can go down, and there are no days to keep up.
  • A one-page summary, if they choose. With softPause+, the person can share one page: their last 30 pauses as before → after, two counts (pauses so far, and how many ended lower than they began), and the line “These are my own answers”. They send it themselves, to whoever they choose, like any photo.

What you will never get

Data from the app. There is no clinician dashboard, no account to link, and nothing is ever sent to you. We have no copy of anyone’s pauses to send. If someone shows you their page, it is because they chose to.

Not for emergencies

softPause is not for a crisis. Anyone in danger should call local emergency services. In the app, Not okay? is always on Home: it shows the emergency number and a support line for the phone’s region, and it works offline.
