One question. Then the app decides.

Choosing a technique is the last thing you want to do while tense. So softPause asks one thing and takes it from there.

01

One question

How do you feel right now? Five levels, from Overwhelmed to Okay. Tap one.

02

The app picks the breath

A few sighs, cyclic sighing or slow breathing, and for how long. A glowing orb and soft haptics pace it, so you can follow with your eyes closed. Stop any time.

03

Before → after

Afterwards, the same question on the same scale. Every pause gets an honest before → after.

04

A record that never punishes

How many pauses so far, how many ended lower than they began. No days to keep up. Nothing on it can go down.
